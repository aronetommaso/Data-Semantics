[EVENT: TUR23378]
Date: 2017-09-26
Country: Turkey | Location: Hakkari
Type: Strategic developments > Change to group/activity | Disorder: Strategic developments
Actor1: Government of Turkey (2016-) [State forces]
Fatalities: 0
Sources: Mezopotamya Agency [National]

DESCRIPTION:
Security measures: On 26 September 2017, Hakkari governor's office declared total 31 zones in Semdinli, Yuksekova, Cukurca and the central districts 'special security zones' due to ongoing security operations, banning citizens' entry until 10 October 2017. On 10 October 2017, the governor's office extended the measure until 25 October 2017. On 10 November 2017, the governor's office declared the 31 zones 'special security zones' again due to ongoing security operations, banning citizens' entry until 25 November 2017. On 25 December 2017, the governor's office declared the 31 zones 'special security zones' yet another time, banning citizens' entry until 8 January 2018 due to security risks. On 5 January 2018, the governor's office declared the 31 zones 'special security zones' again, this time banning civilian entry to and exit from them from 9 January to 23 January 2018.
