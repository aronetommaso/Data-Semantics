[EVENT: IRN19936]
Date: 2022-09-30
Country: Iran | Location: Zahedan
Type: Riots > Violent demonstration | Disorder: Demonstrations
Actor1: Rioters (Iran) [Rioters]
Actor2: Military Forces of Iran (1989-) Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps [State forces]
Fatalities: 103
Sources: Baloch Campaign; BBC News; Haalvash; HRW; Iran Human Rights Documentation Center [Other-National]

DESCRIPTION:
On 30 September 2022, Sunni Baloch worshippers gathered outside a police station in Zahedan city (Sistan and Baluchestan) demanding justice for a young girl allegedly raped by a police chief, amid nationwide unrest over the death of Mahsa Amini. Some demonstrators threw stones and at least two Molotov cocktails at the station. One officer was injured in the leg. Security forces opened fire on the demonstrators, but also on worshipper in the adjacent prayer house, killing and injuring both demonstrators and worshippers. Unrest spread to other parts of the city, with clashes breaking out between people and security forces and several other people were killed. Police cars and banks were set on fire. At one point during the unrest when security forces were shooting at a Sunni mosque and seminary, an armed individual opened fire on IRGC personnel. He had no affiliation to armed groups. Exact fatality counts are unclear, but according to Baloch campaign, at least 97 Balochis and 6 IRGC and Basij officers were killed, with over 300 people injured.
