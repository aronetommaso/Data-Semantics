[EVENT: TUR3592]
Date: 2016-07-15
Country: Turkey | Location: Ankara
Type: Strategic developments > Other | Disorder: Strategic developments
Actor1: Mutiny of Military Forces of Turkey (2016-) [State forces]
Fatalities: 0
Sources: Anadolu Agency; Hurriyet Daily [National]

DESCRIPTION:
Coup / Military Takeover: A group within the Military Forces of Turkey attempted a coup in the night from July 15 to July 16 using weapons and tanks mainly in Istanbul and Ankara to overthrow the running government. All 4 political parties have condemned the coup. It is not clear if the soldiers are affiliated with an outside group. In Ankara the coup plotters naming themselves 'Peace in the Country Council' took over the state-run broadcaster TRT and published a statement on TV stating that it took over power in Turkey. President Erdogan claims that Fetullah Gulen, the religious leader of the Gulen Movement, was behind the coup. Various sources have identified other orchestrators, including some blaming the government itself. The person in the military identified as planning the coup is said to be Muharrem Kose, a colonel in the Turkish military. The head of Turkey's military, General Hulusi Akar was kidnapped and later rescued on July 16 in Ankara. After the coup plotters declared a curfew across the whole country, President Erdogan and Prime Minister Binali Yildirim called people to the streets to resist against the coup. Clashes happened mainly in Ankara and Istanbul between the coup plotters and soldiers and police loyal to the government, and as well as pro-government rioters. The coup was not successful. The Government gained control again with around 700 coup plotters surrendering to the Police.
