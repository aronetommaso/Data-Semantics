Actor1: Protesters (Lebanon) [Protesters]
Fatalities: 0
Sources: Al Nour; AlManar TV; An-Nahar; El Nashra; Megaphone News; Murr TV; National News Agency Lebanon [New media-National]

DESCRIPTION:
Strikes: On 7 January 2021, the 'Committee for Part-time School Teachers' organized a nationwide open strike in protest against the Minister of Education and Higher Education's decision to decrease the number of their classes. The part-time, primary and secondary education teachers, public, vocational, and technical schools' teachers, and Lebanese University teachers went on various strikes in January, February, and March. Some of the teachers' demands included all their hours mentioned in the contracts to be calculated, protesting against the draft budget articles, including those targeting public-sector retirees' benefits and full-time employment of the part-timers. On 20 March 2021, Lebanese University part-time teachers announced their open strike. On 22 March 2021, the association of secondary education teachers suspended its strike. Coded at the Capital, Beirut.
