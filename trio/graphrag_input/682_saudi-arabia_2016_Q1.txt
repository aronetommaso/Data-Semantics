Actor1: Police Forces of Saudi Arabia (2015-) [State forces]
Actor2: Civilians (Yemen) [Civilians]
Fatalities: 47
Sources: AFP; Saudi Press Agency [National-International]

DESCRIPTION:
Saudi Arabia executed 47 people on terrorism charges, representing the biggest mass execution in the country for such offenses since 1980. While most of the suspects are believed to belong to AQAP, including Fares al Shuwail, the top AQAP leader in Saudi Arabia, one of them is a prominent Shiite opposition cleric, Nimr al Nimr. The executions were carried out in 12 different cities across the country.
