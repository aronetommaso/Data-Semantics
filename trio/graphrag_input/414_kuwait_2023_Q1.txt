DESCRIPTION:
Other: On 19 March 2023, the Kuwaiti Constitutional Court invalidated the National Assembly elections held on September 2022, ordering the dissolution of the newly elected parliament and the reinstatement of the previously disbanded one (coded to capital Kuwait City). Based on this ruling, Crown Prince Sheikh Meshal al-Ahmad al-Sabah announced the dissolution of the Assembly and ordered early parliamentary elections. The dissolved parliament was formed by a 28/50 majority of opposition members. This development occurred amid political tensions between the parliament and cabinet that had led to the resignation of the cabinet on 23 January.
