[EVENT: LBN7606]
Date: 2020-10-28
Country: Lebanon | Location: Al Hermel
Type: Strategic developments > Change to group/activity | Disorder: Strategic developments
Actor1: Government of Lebanon (2020-2021) [State forces]
Fatalities: 0
Sources: Al Nour; El Nashra [National]

DESCRIPTION:
Security measures: On 28 October 2020, authorities announced that the city of El Hermel will implement a new set of regulations aimed to curb the spread of the coronavirus. Masks are now obligatory in all areas, all but basic institutions and shops will be closed for at least a week and those allowed to remain open will close between 6 pm and 6 am. Funerals and weddings are forbidden to be held and buses with more than 8 passengers are also not allowed to operate. Three days later on 31 October 2020, authorities announced that the city of Baalbek will observe a strict lockdown from 31 October to 28 November, with the closure of all major institutions during that time, due to an increase in coronavirus cases. Restaurants are allowed to operate at 50% capacity and offer delivery services. However, if coronavirus cases keep increasing, restaurants will be shut down too.
