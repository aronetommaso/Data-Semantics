DESCRIPTION:
On 1 July 2023, two separate armed clashes took place between Tawk clansmen and armed militiamen from Bqaa Safrine at Qurnat as Sawda peak (Al Miniyeh Al Danniyeh), leaving 2 individuals including a Tawk clansman killed.
