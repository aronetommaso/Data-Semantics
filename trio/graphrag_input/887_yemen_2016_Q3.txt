Date: 2016-07-28

Type: Strategic developments > Other | Disorder: Strategic developments
Actor1: Government of Yemen (2015-2017) Houthi-Saleh [State forces]
Fatalities: 0
Sources: AFP; Xinhua [International]

DESCRIPTION:
The Houthi-Saleh alliance signed and announced in Sanaa city the creation of a 10-member Supreme Political Council to 'unify efforts to confront the aggression by Saudi Arabia and its allies' and 'manage state affairs politically, militarily, economically, administratively and in security'. Both the UN Envoy Ould Cheikh Ahmed and the Hadi government denounced the move, calling it a violation of the Yemeni constitution and of UNSC Resolution 2216.
