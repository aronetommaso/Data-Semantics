[EVENT: TUR18169]

Country: Turkey | Location: Tunceli
Type: Strategic developments > Change to group/activity | Disorder: Strategic developments
Actor1: Government of Turkey (2016-) [State forces]
Fatalities: 0
Sources: Haber 7; Mezopotamya Agency; Anadolu Agency [National]

DESCRIPTION:
Security measures: On 29 September 2021, Tunceli Governor's Office issued a ban on all forms of marches, protests, press conferences, sit-ins and other political gatherings in the province for 15 days.
