Other: On 22 October 2022, Iranian IRGC Navy forces seized Panama-flagged ARIANA chemicals tanker (IMO: 9500132) in Omani territorial waters off the Coast of Musandam (Oman) as it was going from Sharjah in the UAE to Al Mukha port in Yemen. IRGC forces brought the tanker to Qeshm island before moving it to Bandar Abbas where they detained its crew of 20 Yemeni and 1 Iraqi and sailors and offloaded its cargo. Iran claimed that the ship engaged in the smuggling of Iranian oil while other sources reported that the oil cargo was Iraqi. The vessel and the crew were still detained as of May 2023.
