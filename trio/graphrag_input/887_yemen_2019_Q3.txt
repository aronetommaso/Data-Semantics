[EVENT: YEM43415]
Date: 2019-09-30
Country: Yemen | Location: Sanaa
Type: Strategic developments > Other | Disorder: Strategic developments
Actor1: Military Forces of Yemen (2017-) Houthi [State forces]
Actor2: Civilians (Yemen) [Civilians]
Fatalities: 0
Sources: Al Masdar; ICRC; Twitter; Yemen Data Project [Local partner-National]

DESCRIPTION:
On 30 September 2019, pro-Houthi forces reportedly released 290 detainees, including 3 Saudis and 42 people who survived an airstrike on a detention centre in Dhamar on September 1, in a unilateral operation confirmed by the ICRC. Some of them had, according to the Mothers Abductees Organization, been subjected to torture but it is not clear where this might have taken place. The release created confidence between the warring sides and decreased political violence in Yemen in the subsequent days.
